# Darling in the Franxx

**Darling in the Franxx** (Japanese: *ダーリン・イン・ザ・フランキス*) is a 2018 Japanese science fiction romance and mecha anime series co-produced by **Trigger** and **A-1 Pictures** (later *CloverWorks*).

> *"If you don't belong here, just build a place where you do. If you don't have a partner, just find another one. If you can't find one, just take one by force!"* — **Zero Two**

---

## 📖 Synopsis
Set in a post-apocalyptic future where humanity resides in fortified mobile cities called **Plantations**, children are raised solely to pilot giant mechs known as **Franxx** in male-female pairs to defend civilization against mysterious giant creatures called **Klaxosaurs**. The story follows **Hiro** (Code:016), a former prodigy who struggles to pilot, until he meets **Zero Two** (Code:002), an elite pilot with klaxosaur blood who calls him her *"Darling."*
